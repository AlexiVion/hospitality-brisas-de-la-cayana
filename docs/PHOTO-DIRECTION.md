# Photo Direction

## Purpose

Photography is the primary emotional system of Brisas de la Cayana. The website should not behave like a listing that dumps every available image into a gallery. Each photograph needs a narrative role.

## Core rule

**Do not use a photo because it is good. Use it because it does a job in the story.**

The first release should use roughly 15–20 primary images throughout the long-form page, with an optional secondary gallery for additional approved images.

## Image families

### 1. Hero / impact

Goal: establish Brisas as a refuge inside a much larger landscape.

Best characteristics:

- cabin visible but not dominant
- large amount of mountain / sky / vegetation
- strong sense of scale
- horizontal crop that supports headline placement

Primary candidate direction: the cabin isolated in the landscape under a clear blue sky.

## 2. Arrival / transition

Goal: communicate leaving the city and entering the sierras.

Subjects:

- mountain road
- approach to Villa Alpina
- entrance signage
- trail / path

Use sparingly. Arrival photography works well near the beginning or practical arrival section.

## 3. Landscape / place

Goal: show that the environment is the main reason to come.

Subjects:

- wide mountain views
- creek
- trees
- valley
- fog / clouds
- winter conditions
- sunset
- open sky

Do not force all seasons into one visual color grade. The changing landscape is part of the product.

## 4. Cabin / refuge

Goal: prove that the accommodation is comfortable and understandable.

Subjects:

- exterior
- deck / veranda
- bedroom
- kitchen
- bathroom
- dining / sitting area
- windows and view relationships

Use fewer, stronger frames rather than showing every angle.

## 5. Experience / activity

Goal: help the visitor imagine being there.

Strong existing themes:

- horseback riding
- walking
- creek exploration
- trails
- outdoor meals / drinks
- sitting outside
- mountain views

Prefer images that feel observed rather than staged.

## 6. Stillness / atmosphere

Goal: sell the option of doing nothing.

Strong motifs:

- white chairs facing nature
- empty deck
- quiet table
- fog
- evening light
- distant cabin
- sunset silhouettes

These images should be paired with very little copy.

## 7. Practical / orientation

Goal: make arrival and remote-stay expectations concrete.

Potential subjects:

- road / access
- property entrance
- parking
- solar / infrastructure elements when visually appropriate
- map / location context

These photos are functional, not necessarily portfolio hero images.

## Proposed narrative image sequence

```text
01 Hero
   cabin in landscape

02 Arrival
   road into the sierras

03 Slow down
   wide landscape / creek

04 Refuge
   cabin exterior

05 Refuge detail
   deck or interior

06 Pause
   white chairs facing landscape

07 Experience
   horseback riding

08 Experience
   creek / walking

09 Experience
   food or drink with view

10 Stillness
   empty deck / mist

11 Story
   landscape / property context

12 Practical
   access / arrival image

13 Gallery transition
   asymmetric image pair

14 Location
   wide valley / signage / map support

15 Closing
   sunset / evening silhouette
```

## Hero selection criteria

A hero candidate should score well on:

- recognizability of place
- emotional impact
- crop flexibility
- enough negative space for text
- high original resolution
- strong desktop and mobile variants
- no embedded text / logos

If the best desktop image crops badly on mobile, use separate art-directed crops rather than forcing one file everywhere.

## Original asset request

For final implementation, request from the owner:

- original image files
- highest available resolution
- no Instagram / Booking / Airbnb screenshots
- no added text overlays
- no social watermarks if clean originals exist

Suggested message:

> ¿Me podrías pasar las fotos originales que usás en Booking, Airbnb e Instagram? Para la demo quiero trabajarlas en máxima calidad y sin el texto de las publicaciones.

## Curation rules

Reject or de-prioritize:

- duplicates
- near-duplicates
- visibly compressed screenshots
- images with large text overlays
- images dominated by platform UI
- accidental / weak framing
- photographs that reveal irrelevant private information
- images whose quality is not sufficient for the intended display size

## Editing rules

Allowed:

- crop
- slight exposure correction
- restrained color / contrast balancing
- modern web-format conversion

Avoid:

- sky replacement
- object insertion
- dramatic HDR
- artificial sunset effects
- heavy presets
- AI reconstruction of the actual property

The website should preserve documentary credibility.

## Alt-text policy

Use descriptive alt text when a photograph conveys content needed to understand the property.

Examples:

```text
"Brisas de la Cayana cabin surrounded by mountain vegetation in Villa Alpina"
"Rocky creek running through the landscape near the cabin"
```

Use empty alt text for purely decorative atmospheric images when the same information is already conveyed in adjacent content.
