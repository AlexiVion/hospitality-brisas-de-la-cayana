# Project Brief

## Business context

Brisas de la Cayana is a real cabin accommodation in Villa Alpina, Córdoba, Argentina. Its current digital presence is distributed across Instagram, WhatsApp, Airbnb, Booking.com and Google Maps rather than centered on a strong owned website.

The accommodation already has unusually strong raw material for a direct web experience: a distinctive location, abundant photography, an authentic host story, mountain and creek scenery, outdoor activities, and a clear sense of place.

This project is a **prospect demo** for a real business. It is not presented as completed paid client work unless that commercial relationship is later established.

## Primary problem

Brisas has a compelling product and rich visual content, but no owned web experience that turns those assets into a coherent story and a clear path toward direct contact or booking.

## Strategic opportunity

The website should not compete with Booking.com or Airbnb by recreating an OTA listing. Its advantage is to do what those platforms cannot do as well:

- express the identity of Brisas
- tell the story behind the place
- use photography editorially rather than as a grid of listing images
- explain the experience of Villa Alpina
- set expectations honestly before arrival
- provide a direct relationship with the host
- create a clean boundary for future booking-engine integration

## Target user

Primary visitors are leisure travellers, couples and small groups considering a quiet mountain escape in the Sierras de Córdoba.

They may arrive through Instagram, Google, a referral, Booking.com, Airbnb or social content. Before contacting the host, they need to understand:

- where the property is
- what staying there feels like
- what the cabin includes
- what they can do nearby
- what practical constraints come with the remote setting
- whether it suits their group
- how to check availability or book

The target is defined by travel intent rather than by a narrow demographic profile.

## Business objective

The primary action is:

**Consult availability directly.**

The initial demo should collect arrival date, departure date and guest count, then open WhatsApp with a structured message containing those details.

Secondary actions are:

- explore the property and gallery
- understand location and arrival conditions
- open the existing Airbnb listing
- open the existing Booking.com listing
- view Instagram
- open Google Maps

## Narrative objective

The site should move the visitor through an emotional and practical progression:

```text
Arrive
  -> discover the landscape
  -> imagine being there
  -> understand the cabin
  -> experience the place
  -> discover the story
  -> understand the practical realities
  -> trust
  -> enquire
```

The visitor should feel that the landscape is the main product and the cabin is the refuge within it.

## Positioning

The desired positioning is:

**A quiet, authentic mountain refuge in Villa Alpina where the experience is defined by landscape, creek, open sky, slow time and simple comfort.**

Avoid presenting Brisas as a generic luxury lodge, a polished urban hotel, or a catalog of amenities.

## Content pillars

### 1. The landscape

Mountains, creek, weather, paths, vegetation, changing light and sky.

### 2. The refuge

The cabin, deck, interiors, views, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom, outdoor seating and practical comforts.

### 3. The experience

Walking, horseback riding, quiet meals, looking at the stars, sitting outside, exploring the creek, or simply doing nothing.

### 4. The story

The host's long relationship with Villa Alpina and the origin of Brisas de la Cayana should humanize the property without becoming an overly long biography.

### 5. Practical honesty

Remote hospitality works better when expectations are clear. Arrival timing, distance from shops, energy constraints, fire care and other relevant conditions should be explained calmly and positively rather than hidden.

## Scope

Initial release:

- one long-form responsive landing page
- editorial storytelling structure
- photo-led hero
- cabin overview
- experience / activity sections
- host / origin story
- amenities
- practical arrival information
- photo gallery
- location section
- real review / trust section when approved and attributable
- availability enquiry form
- WhatsApp deep link with structured message
- secondary Airbnb / Booking.com links
- social and map links
- SEO metadata
- responsive behavior for mobile, tablet and desktop
- accessibility basics

Supporting portfolio documentation:

- project metadata
- UX strategy
- visual direction
- photo direction
- full first-pass copy
- wireframe
- implementation boundary
- case-study draft

## Non-goals

The prospect demo intentionally does not claim or implement:

- real-time inventory unless a valid source is connected
- payment processing
- reservation confirmation
- PMS functionality
- channel-management functionality
- OTA synchronization
- fabricated reviews
- fabricated availability
- fabricated prices
- fabricated business results

If the business already uses a booking engine, PMS, channel manager or calendar synchronization system, the production implementation should integrate with that existing layer rather than replace it without reason.

## Source and asset policy

The business is real. Permanent public portfolio use of owner-controlled assets such as original photography, logos, testimonials and private business information should remain subject to appropriate permission.

For the demo build, original high-resolution photographs should be preferred over screenshots or compressed copies from social/OTA platforms.

## Success criteria

The first version is successful if:

- a visitor understands the emotional proposition within the first screen
- the site feels specific to Brisas rather than reusable for any cabin
- the cabin and key practical information are easy to understand
- photography has a narrative role rather than functioning as filler
- the primary CTA remains visible without interrupting the story
- mobile visitors can reach WhatsApp with dates and guest count in a few steps
- secondary OTA links are available without dominating the experience
- practical limitations are communicated clearly and respectfully
- no production integration or commercial result is falsely implied
- the site can be deployed on ClickDialog infrastructure and later connected to a real booking system if needed
